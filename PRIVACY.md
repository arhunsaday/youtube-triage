# Privacy

YouTube Triage collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere except youtube.com.

It reads your Watch Later playlist and removes, re-adds or reorders videos in it only
when you swipe, press a key or click undo. Those requests go from your YouTube tab to
youtube.com with the session you are already signed in with. Your cookies are never
stored, copied or sent elsewhere.

The extension keeps no history and stores no data. Closing the overlay forgets
everything it showed you.
